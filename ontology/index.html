Abstract

The Coconut Ontology contains the data model of Coconut Knowledge Graph. It facilitates the knowledge graph data manipulation and consumption through concepts such as natural products, molecules, and their respective properties.

coco: CoconutKG: Description back to ToC

COCONUT[KG] is a knowledge graph based on the Coconut Dataset, which is a comprehensive platform that facilitates natural product research by providing data, tools, and other resources and provides services for the deposition, curation, and reuse of data.
